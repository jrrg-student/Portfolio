Rockfish

A fishing game about keeping your catch on the line.

Play Rockfish ↗Playable in your browser on itch.io.

About

Rockfish is a rapid prototype of a fishing game built around a skill-based reeling challenge. Cast your line, navigate obstacles, and manage line tension to bring in your catch. Catching Rockfish unlocks new fishing locations with different course behavior.

What I built

I handled the game design and programming in Unity. The main mechanic combines obstacle avoidance with line tension: players steer with the mouse and use the scroll wheel to adjust the fish’s size. Reeling in a fish means balancing both without hitting an obstacle or breaking the line.

I also built the course-generation system for the reeling challenges, with different course behavior across fishing locations.

Continuing development

This build is a rapid prototype, and I plan to keep developing Rockfish beyond it.

The Legend of Zelda Remake + Armor Lock

Play on itch.io In game, the Triforce at the end of the dungeon (or 4) takes you to the custom level. Shift is Armor Lock.

About

A recreation of the first dungeon of the original Legend of Zelda (NES) in Unity: grid-locked movement, the dungeon's enemies and boss, items like the map and compass, keys and locked doors, and the NES heads-up display. After the classic dungeon comes a custom level built around a new mechanic, Armor Lock.

Armor Lock. Hold Shift and Link plants his fist and raises a bubble shield. While it's up nothing can hurt him, fireballs bounce back at whoever threw them, and rolling Digdoggers blow up against it. It only lasts a few seconds and has to recharge, so choosing when to lock is the level's central decision.

What I built

  • The project's foundation: the NES aspect ratio, the dungeon scenes, and Link's four-direction sprites.
  • Health and damage: Link's hearts on the HUD, knockback, and the red flash when an enemy is hit.
  • Room transitions, and, with my teammate Evan, the keys and locked doors, including keys that only appear once a room's enemies are defeated, placed where the original has them.
  • Enemies: the Goriya and its boomerang, the Keese with a reusable sprite-oscillation component, the Gel, enemy spawning, and, with Evan, the Wallmaster and blade traps. The blade traps wait for Link to line up with them, strike, and slide back home, like the NES.
  • Items and puzzle rooms: the boomerang pickup, pushable blocks, the Old Man room that opens on a push, and the side-view bow room.
  • Audio: the background music and sound effects, including the Armor Lock sounds.
  • All of the bug fixes and polish for the final build, like the sword beam timing, Link's and Aquamentus's hitboxes, and enemy animations that stopped after respawning.
